**Figure 3.** \[DC11.1]\[PL11.2]Density distributions of (A) daily mean pairwise distances per individual (m), (B) 1-hour mean flock densities (1/m2), (C) individual normalised daily distance travelled (m), and (D) individual normalised daily space use (m2). Colours in (A), (C) and (D) represent different individuals, whereas in (B) they represent different days. Data were collected from 16 Welsh Mountain ewes, at the Henfaes Research Centre, U.K.



**Figure 4.** Effect of drone presence on (A) normalised hourly distance travelled (m), (B) daily space use (m2) within the 50% utilization distribution, and (C) 1-hour mean flock densities (1/m2). Boxes show medians (solid line), and lower and upper quartiles and whiskers indicate 1.5 x the interquartile range. Black points represent observed data, and white points and bars show the predicted effect with the 95% confidence interval. Data were collected from 16 Welsh Mountain ewes, at the Henfaes Research Centre, U.K.



**Figure 5.** (A) Faecal cortisol metabolite concentrations (ng/g) during the first three weeks of the study. Black points represent log-transformed observed data, and white points and bars show the predicted effect ± SE in the log-scale. Habituation= the first week after the flock was introduced to the study field; Baseline= the week after GPS-collar fitting; Drone= the first week of drone presence. (B) Difference in wool cortisol concentrations (pg/mg) between the start and end of the study. Black points represent the observed data, paired by individual. The white crosses indicate the mean concentration. Boxes show medians (solid line), and lower and upper quartiles and whiskers indicate 1.5 x the interquartile range. Data were collected from 20 Welsh Mountain ewes, at the Henfaes Research Centre, U.K.

Effect of rainfall (absent = 0.0 mm, present > 0 mm) on (C) hourly mean individual pairwise distances (m), and (D) 1-hour mean flock densities (1/m2).
